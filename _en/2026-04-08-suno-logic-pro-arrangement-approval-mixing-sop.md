> Disclaimer: The following is for music production hobbyists' reference only. It summarizes my personal experience: assuming I **already have original lyrics and composition** and **sing the vocals myself**, I use SUNO and Logic Pro for arrangement assistance and adjust my mixing workflow so I don't lose the copyright in my lyrics and composition because of the default AI song. Do not use this method for anything illegal or that infringes copyright.

**Core logic:**
Use "physical analog coloration" to mask the digital characteristics of AI audio (checkerboard noise, high-frequency cliffs, invisible watermarks), and also keep the "AI backing track" and "real human recordings" physically separate at the bus level, creating the acoustic illusion of a "real studio-made backing track + real vocals".

## 📍 Stage 1: Material cleanup (clearing landmines)

> **Goal:** Remove the "problem areas" most likely to trigger AI detection.

1. **Import the stems:** Import the individual instrument stems generated by Suno or another AI tool into Logic Pro.
2. **Delete the AI vocals outright (fatal landmine):** AI-generated lead and backing vocals sound strongly metallic and synthetic, with phase defects. They **must be fully deleted, muted, or discarded outright**, and replaced entirely with vocals and harmonies you recorded yourself.
3. **(Optional, advanced) Drum doubling:** If the AI drums sound too digital, select the drum track and use `Track -> Drum Replacement/Doubling` to layer on a real drum machine sample built into Logic.

## 📍 Stage 2: Group isolation (core architecture)

> **Goal:** Prevent AI backing-track processing from "collaterally damaging" the clean human vocals.

1. Select all AI-generated instrument tracks (woodwinds, strings, synths, guitar, bass, drums, etc.).
2. Press `Command + Shift + D`.
3. Choose **Summing Stack** and click Create.
4. Rename the new main track to **"AI Backing Bus"**.
5. **Note:** All vocals and live instruments you recorded yourself **must stay outside this bus** and go directly to Stereo Out.

## 📍 Stage 3: Physical analog coloration (breaking the AI watermark)

> **Goal:** Add analog-circuit harmonics to the backing bus to break up the regularity of the AI spectrum.

In the Audio FX chain of the **"AI Backing Bus"**, insert `Phat FX` and set the parameters as follows:

1. **BANDPASS:** Completely off (greyed out).
2. **DISTORTION:** On.
   * `Bit Crush`: 0.0%
   * `Sat`: 10% - 15%
   * `Vari Drive`: choose `Tube` or `Soft`, push to around 5%.
3. **BASS ENHANCER:** On.
   * `Type`: Warm
   * `Amount`: 10% - 15% (disrupts the low-frequency phase)
4. **COMPRESSOR:** On.
   * `Type`: Vintage VCA (adds hardware nonlinearity)
   * `Amount`: 15% - 20%
5. **MASTER (main output mix) - the most critical:**
   * Turn the `Mix` knob to **25% - 30%**.
   * *(Note: use just 30% analog coloration to dirty up the background while keeping 70% of the original clarity.)*

## 📍 Stage 4: High-frequency cliff repair (fooling the spectrum analyzer)

> **Goal:** Fix the cliff where the AI backing track is cut off above 18kHz.

On the **"AI Backing Bus"**, right after `Phat FX`, insert a `Channel EQ`:
1. Turn on the **High Cut / Low Pass** filter on the far right.
2. Set the cutoff frequency to around **17500 Hz**.
3. Set the slope to the gentlest setting, **12dB/Oct**.
   * *(Note: artificially recreate the natural high-frequency rolloff of old gear to mask the unnatural drop-off caused by insufficient AI compute.)*

## 📍 Stage 5: Mixing and export

1. **Vocal blending:** On the Stereo Out bus, insert your usual mastering chain (a bit of bus glue compression, a limiter, etc.) so the clean human vocals blend naturally with the backing track's "physical patina".
2. **No need to change BPM:** Once you apply the bus coloration above, the AI's acoustic fingerprint is completely covered. There's no need to force a slight BPM adjustment across the whole song (to avoid severe track misalignment when Flex is not enabled).
3. **Export format:** Bounce it as usual to a 24-bit / 48kHz WAV file.

## 💡 Notes and summary
For audio exported with this workflow, the detection algorithm will read it as: **"extremely realistic vocals + a backing track with the studio character of analog hardware"**, so it's judged to be traditional human music production and passes AI detection.
